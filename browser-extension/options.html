Aether Download Helper

Right-click a link, video or audio element and choose “Download with Aether”.


  

  

    
Cookies

    Off by default. When enabled, Aether attaches your cookies for the
      downloaded URL so authenticated/intranet links work. Cookies are only
      sent with the download request and never stored.
    
    

  

  

    
Native host manifest must be installed for this extension ID. See
      browser-extension/README.md and
      docs/BROWSER_INTEGRATION.md.
